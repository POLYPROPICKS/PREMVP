- **Settlement:** the existing provider resolver (`resolveSignalOutcome`). 3,281 conditions looked up, 0 source-unavailable. States: SETTLED_WIN 3,237 · SETTLED_LOSS 3,237 ·
  UNRESOLVED 44 · **IDENTITY_NOT_PROVEN 88** · no VOID. Unresolved is never a loss (none of the 44 carries a PnL).
- **Identity finding (22 provider markets, unchanged):** 22 `condition_id`s (88 sibling rows) appear under **two different physical events**. One market settles once, so its
  exposure cannot be assigned to one physical event; both attributions are IDENTITY_NOT_PROVEN and excluded (counted). Verified on the materialized table: all 88 rows of
  multi-event conditions are IDENTITY_NOT_PROVEN, no other row is, and 0 selected exposures (any view / model) repeat a condition or a physical event.
- **One common physical-event denominator: 148 events** (>= 1 settled, priced supported sibling), used by every model and view.
- **Frozen engine, unchanged:** `lib/modeling/research-engine` (C0 = 0.50 <= entry < 0.60; C1 adds soccer). One selected bet per physical event,
  chronologically first qualifying candidate; ties resolve to the **lowest entry price**. Selection runs over settled, priced siblings only.
- **Three separate views (never merged):**
  - `RAW` — every sibling priced at its T10 best ask, ignoring the 0.54 cap, min order size and depth. **Not an executable return.**
  - `EXECUTABLE_UPPER_BOUND` — siblings proven executable by telemetry plus siblings whose executability is unknown *only* for lack of persisted evidence. Conclusively
    blocked siblings and telemetry failures are excluded. Depth / VWAP / fee are unknown on pre-telemetry rows: an upper bound.
  - `EXECUTABLE_PROVEN` — only rows whose `T10_EXECUTABLE_SIBLING_TELEMETRY_V1` state is EXECUTABLE: **103 siblings, 1 selected bet** in this range.
- **Telemetry in range:** 220 telemetry rows (fee known 106) in the requested range. The clone holds 352 telemetry rows in total; the other 132 belong to events starting on or

## Reading it honestly

1. **No positive edge is visible** in this window. Even the uncollapsed all-sibling view is negative (SPREADS weakest). Sampling error is large: the standard error of a flat-1u
   ROI on N=138 is about 9 pp (roughly +/-17 pp at 95%), so this is "no evidence of an edge", not a proven negative.
2. **RAW overstates what is tradable.** 92 of the 138 RAW C0 selections are conclusively not executable at T10 under the live invariants (56 above the 0.54 cap, 36 under the
   venue minimum at $2.50); 46 remain in the upper bound and only 1 is proven executable (a single loss, -1u: **not a statistic**).
3. **Five days, soccer-dominated, one capture per event.** Not enough to rank models; enough to size the executable gap and validate the pipeline.
4. Fee: known only on telemetry rows (3 of 138 RAW C0 selections); PnL is gross.
5. `EXECUTABLE_PROVEN` is still too small to read; it grows as post-release T10 cohorts settle (132 telemetry rows for events from 2026-10-06 onward are not yet included).
