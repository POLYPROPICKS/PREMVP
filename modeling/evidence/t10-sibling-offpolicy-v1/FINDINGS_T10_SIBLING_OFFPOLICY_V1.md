## What this is — and is not

- **Population:** every supported (MONEYLINE / SPREADS / TOTALS / TOTAL_CORNERS) sibling captured at `T_MINUS_10` for events starting
  2026-10-01..2026-10-05: **6,342 siblings, 145 physical events** (MONEYLINE 870, SPREADS 2,880, TOTALS 2,592). One T10 capture per event
  (checked: 0 events with more than one T10 capture time, 0 duplicate exact identities). All 145 events are `soccer` (frozen sport authority
  `research_evidence_page_rows.provider_sport_family`), so **C0, C1, C4 and C5 select identically** here; C1/C4/C5 add no information over C0.
- **Settlement:** the existing provider resolver (`resolveSignalOutcome`; Gamma's list endpoint returned nothing for these conditions, so the resolver's
  CLOB fallback decided). 3,149 conditions looked up, 0 source-unavailable. States: SETTLED_WIN 3,039 · SETTLED_LOSS 3,039 · UNRESOLVED 176 ·
  **IDENTITY_NOT_PROVEN 88** · no VOID. Independent spot-check against the CLOB `tokens[].winner` flag: 12/12 agree. Unresolved is never a loss.
- **Identity finding (22 provider markets):** 22 `condition_id`s (88 sibling rows: spread and total lines of Once Caldas / Internacional de Bogota) appear under
  **two different physical events** — `provider:polymarket:1044235:2026-10-02` and `provider:polymarket:game:90111079:2026-10-04`. One market settles once, so its
  exposure cannot be assigned to one physical event; both attributions are marked IDENTITY_NOT_PROVEN and excluded (before this guard one market was selected under both
  events, i.e. double-counted). This is an upstream physical-event identity inconsistency worth its own look.
- **One common physical-event denominator: 139 events** (>= 1 settled, priced supported sibling), used by every model and view.
- **Frozen engine, unchanged:** `lib/modeling/research-engine` (C0 = 0.50 <= entry < 0.60; C1 adds soccer). One selected bet per physical event,
  chronologically first qualifying candidate; all siblings of an event share one T10 capture time, so ties resolve to the **lowest entry price**.
  Selection runs over settled, priced siblings only, so an unresolved lower-priced sibling cannot be chosen and may shift which sibling a model picks.
- **Three separate views (never merged):**
  - `RAW` — every sibling priced at its T10 best ask, ignoring the 0.54 cap, min order size and depth. **Not an executable return.**
  - `EXECUTABLE_UPPER_BOUND` — siblings proven executable by telemetry plus siblings whose executability is unknown *only* for lack of persisted evidence
    (ask-ladder depth; before 2026-10-03 also the minimum order size). Conclusively blocked siblings (best ask above 0.54; $2.50 below the venue minimum at the best
    ask) and telemetry failures are excluded. Depth / VWAP / fee are unknown there: an upper bound.
  - `EXECUTABLE_PROVEN` — only rows whose `T10_EXECUTABLE_SIBLING_TELEMETRY_V1` state is EXECUTABLE: **0** (the telemetry is not released yet).

## Reading it honestly

1. **No positive edge is visible** in this window. Even the uncollapsed all-sibling view is negative (SPREADS weakest). Sampling error is large: the standard error of a flat-1u
   ROI on N=129 is about 9 pp (roughly +/-17 pp at 95%), so this is "no evidence of an edge", not a proven negative.
2. **RAW overstates what is tradable.** 86 of the 129 RAW C0/C1 selections are conclusively not executable at T10 under the live invariants (54 above the 0.54 cap, 32 under the
   venue minimum at $2.50); only 43 remain in the upper bound, and none can yet be proven executable.
3. **Five days, soccer only, one capture per event.** Not enough to rank models; enough to size the executable gap and validate the pipeline.
4. Fee: 100% UNKNOWN (gross only) until telemetry rows exist.
