## Primary decision questions

1. **Can selective Soccer widening increase P&L above +379.30u?** Yes. QUALITY_FILL_A (Tennis P50_52 →
   Soccer P50_54 → remaining P50_52) reaches **+402.25u** at cap 50, +22.95u / +6.05% above the P50_52
   baseline, using only the proven Soccer .52–.54 sleeve (+63.64u, 30.60% ROI standalone) instead of widening
   every sport to .54.
2. **Does putting Esports last improve capped P&L without sacrificing sparse-day fill?** No net gain over
   QUALITY_FILL_A: QUALITY_FILL_B (+400.25u) is marginally below QUALITY_FILL_A (+402.25u) at cap 50 — moving
   Esports from "excluded" to "last tier" costs ~2u once Esports actually starts filling cap 50 slots (see
   TABLE 2, QUALITY_FILL_B's 41–50 layer ROI drops to 19.56%, dragged by Esports P50_52's known -2.48%
   standalone ROI). Fill rate is identical to QUALITY_FILL_A (0.5000/0.3750/0.3125) at this cap range — the
   Esports tier does not yet get exercised meaningfully within cap 50 on this dataset.
3. **Does Soccer 0.54–0.60 add useful last-resort capacity at cap50?** Yes for fill quality, at a P&L cost:
   QUALITY_FILL_C reaches the best fill rates of all 5 models tested (Fill30 0.5833, Fill50 0.3333, tying
   PORTFOLIO_BROAD) but its cap50 P&L (+396.76u) is below both QUALITY_FILL_A and QUALITY_FILL_B — the
   0.54–0.60 layer's lower standalone ROI (9.70%–3.24%/7.02% for the individual .54–.60 soccer cells) drags
   the 41–50 layer down to 18.36% ROI.
4. **Which candidate produces the highest absolute P&L AND 30/40/50-day fill quality?** No single candidate
   wins both outright. **QUALITY_FILL_A has the highest absolute P&L** (+402.25u @ cap50). **QUALITY_FILL_C
   has the best fill quality** (Fill30/40/50 = 0.5833/0.3958/0.3333). QUALITY_FILL_B sits between the two on
   both axes and does not lead on either.
5. **Is there evidence 50/day can be reached more often without sacrificing marginal P&L?** Partially.
   QUALITY_FILL_C raises Fill50 from P50_52's 0.2917 to 0.3333 (+14.3% relative) while still beating P50_52's
   cap50 P&L by +17.46u — so yes, some additional fill can be bought without going negative. But the
   highest-fill candidate (QUALITY_FILL_C) is not the highest-P&L candidate (QUALITY_FILL_A) — maximizing
   both simultaneously is not supported by this fixed candidate set; the two objectives trade off past
   QUALITY_FILL_A's tier 3.

- **BEST_OBSERVED_PNL:** QUALITY_FILL_A @ cap 50 — +402.25u (ROI 29.11%, MaxDD -28.62u), +22.95u above the
  corrected P50_52 @ cap50 baseline (+379.30u) and +27.37u above PORTFOLIO_BROAD @ cap50 (+374.88u).
- **BEST_30_DAY_PROJECTION:** QUALITY_FILL_A @ cap 50 — 251.41u flat-1u research projection (highest of all
  5 models × 3 caps tested).
- **BEST_FILL_QUALITY:** QUALITY_FILL_C — Fill30/40/50 = 0.5833/0.3958/0.3333, the best of all 5 candidates,
  achieved by using Soccer 0.54–0.60 strictly as a last-resort tier rather than a blanket widening.
- **DOES_SELECTIVE_WIDENING_ADD_VALUE:** Yes. All three QUALITY_FILL candidates beat both the corrected
  P50_52 baseline and PORTFOLIO_BROAD in absolute cap50 P&L (TABLE 4). Selective Soccer-only widening
  (QUALITY_FILL_A) captures the value of the proven .52–.54 soccer sleeve without diluting it across every
  sport the way PORTFOLIO_BROAD's blanket P52_54 tier does (PORTFOLIO_BROAD is actually *below* the P50_52
  baseline at cap50, -4.42u). Pushing Esports last (QUALITY_FILL_B) and adding Soccer 0.54–0.60 last
  (QUALITY_FILL_C) both trade some P&L for fill quality rather than improving both simultaneously.
